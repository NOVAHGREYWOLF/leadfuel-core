Merge desk

Every open PR in every repo, and every queued desk task that has no PR yet. Buttons only record what you want. Nothing merges, readies a draft or messages anyone from a click here.

Times are Pacific. ETA is the live slot-queue position times the measured median CI time for that repo. It reads unknown when the feeder cannot measure it. Intent rows are described in docs/MERGE_DESK_INTENTS.md in leadfuel-core.
