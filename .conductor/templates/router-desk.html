Answers save the moment you click. The router reads them the next time it checks, which is when you message it, and then acts and writes what it did under the question.

Only things that need your go are listed: flag flips, sends, spends, deploy-configuration PRs and desk gates. Anything a desk can do itself never appears. Go or Hold only records your answer. The owning router acts on it and then marks the item done.

Only tasks in this queue get started. The router and the conductor do not pick work on their own. Add a task of your own below, or choose from All tasks.

Statuses come from the build board and can lag. A Router check line is something the router confirmed with GitHub, with the time. Nothing here is marked done for you.
